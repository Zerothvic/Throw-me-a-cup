Not a Liter, Not a Gallon — Just a Cup

Did you know that only 1 in 4 people worldwide have reliable access to safe, drinkable water? Lack of clean water remains one of the world's most urgent challenges. Millions of people, especially in developing regions, struggle daily to access safe water for drinking, cooking, and hygiene.

Contamination from industrial waste, agricultural runoff, and inadequate sanitation systems worsens the crisis, while climate change intensifies droughts and disrupts water supplies, placing additional strain on vulnerable communities. Without clean water, health risks like dehydration and waterborne disease increase dramatically.

Access to safe water is a fundamental human right, essential for life and dignity. Addressing this global issue requires sustainable infrastructure, responsible resource management, and international cooperation.

Who We Are

Problem

Lack of clean, drinkable water threatens global health and development, leaving millions vulnerable to disease, poverty, and environmental insecurity.

Solution

Providing affordable filtration systems, investing in water research, and running community sensitization campaigns can significantly improve access to safe, drinkable water worldwide.

Vision

At Throw Me A Cup, we envision a world where every community can access clean water through effective filtration, continuous research, and awareness campaigns that ensure lasting health.
